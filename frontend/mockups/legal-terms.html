The terms that govern tax preparation, consultations and use of the client portal with Okojie & Associates, Inc.

01Scope of engagement

We prepare federal, state and local income tax returns and provide planning and consultation, based on the information and documents you give us.

02Your responsibilities

You confirm that the information you provide is true, complete and accurate. Under federal law, you remain responsible for the accuracy of your return.

03E-signature and Form 8879

Following IRS Publication 1345, we can’t e-file your return until you’ve reviewed it and signed Form 8879 in your portal.

04Invoices, payments and refunds

Fees depend on your return’s complexity and your tier. Invoices are paid in full before we e-file.
