We protect your confidential financial and tax information under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) and Internal Revenue Code Section 7216.

01Information we collect

The personal information we need to prepare and file your returns: identification numbers, wage and income statements, bank details for direct deposit, and dependent records.

02IRC Section 7216 consent

Federal law restricts how tax preparers use and disclose return information. We never use or share it for anything other than preparing your return without your written consent.

03How we share information

We never sell, rent or lease client information. We share it only with the IRS and state tax agencies once you sign your e-file authorization (Form 8879), or when the law requires it.

04Record retention and disposal

We keep client files as IRS professional standards and statutes of limitations require, then securely destroy them.
