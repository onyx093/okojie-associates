The administrative, technical and physical safeguards we use to protect your information, following IRS Publication 4557 and the FTC Safeguards Rule.

01Written information security plan

We maintain a formal WISP and a named security coordinator who oversees risk assessments, staff training and access controls.

02Encryption and storage

Tax data, returns and identity documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, and only you and your preparer can open your files.

03Multi-factor authentication

Every portal account requires a one-time code from an authenticator app or a passkey, in addition to a password.

04Incident response

We keep an incident response plan for detecting, containing and reporting security events, and we notify affected clients and authorities as the law requires.
