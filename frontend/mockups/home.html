FAQ

Questions, answered

Filing online, security, and fees — here’s how it works.

Do I have to come into an office?

No. Upload & Go and Premium Remote are fully remote: you upload documents to your secure portal, review by video or phone, and e-sign. Hybrid lets you drop off documents or meet in person at one of our Philadelphia or Baltimore offices if you prefer.

How do I send my documents securely?

Through your private client portal. Files are encrypted in transit and at rest and are visible only to you and your preparer. Please don’t send W-2s, IDs, or Social Security numbers by regular email or text.

Is the e-signature legally valid?

Yes. We use IRS Form 8879 (IRS e-file Signature Authorization), which the IRS permits for electronically filed returns. You’ll review your return and sign electronically in your portal before we file.

How much will my return cost?

Fees start in these ranges by tier — Upload & Go $150–$225, Hybrid $225–$350, Premium Remote $400–$900 — and depend on the complexity of your return. You’ll always know your exact price before we file.

Can you help with back taxes or an IRS notice?

Yes. Our IRS notice response add-on covers reading the notice, responding on your behalf, and keeping you informed. We can also prepare prior-year returns and amendments.

Do you serve clients outside Philadelphia and Baltimore?

Yes. Our remote tiers work anywhere in the U.S., and Premium Remote supports multi-state filings including PA, MD, NJ, and DE.

How is my personal information protected?

We follow IRS Publication 4557 and the FTC Safeguards Rule under a written information security plan (WISP). Every client account is protected with multi-factor authentication, and access to your files is limited and logged.

When will I get my refund?

After we e-file, the IRS issues most refunds within about 21 days if there are no issues; direct deposit is fastest. Timing can vary with state processing, credits claimed (such as EITC/ACTC), and any IRS review.
