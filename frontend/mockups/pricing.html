FAQ

Pricing questions

Clear answers about fees, payments and billing.

How much will my return cost?

Fees start in these ranges by tier: Upload & Go $150–$225, Hybrid $225–$350, Premium Remote $400–$900. They depend on the complexity of your return, and you’ll always know your exact price before we file.

When do I pay?

After your preparer sets your fee and before we e-file. You can pay by card in your portal or in person at any office.

Can you help with back taxes or an IRS notice?

Yes. Our IRS notice response add-on covers reading the notice, responding on your behalf, and keeping you informed. We can also prepare prior-year returns and amendments.
